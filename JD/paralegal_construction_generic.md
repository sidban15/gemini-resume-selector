# Job Description: Paralegal (Construction)

**Company:** Mid-Size Construction Firm
**Location:** Remote / On-site
**Status:** Open

## Overview
We are looking for a Paralegal to join our team. We are a mid-size construction company handling various projects across the region. The ideal candidate will help with legal things and make sure our paperwork is in order.

## Responsibilities
* Help the legal team with whatever comes up.
* Look over contracts and construction documents.
* Keep track of deadlines and important dates.
* Deal with liens, insurance, and maybe some litigation stuff.
* Help out with administrative tasks in the office.
* Other duties as assigned.

## Requirements
* Some experience as a paralegal or in a law office.
* Knowledge of construction or real estate is a plus.
* Good at using computers and Microsoft Office.
* Able to work independently when needed.
* High school diploma or equivalent; some college preferred.
* Paralegal certificate is nice to have but not strictly required if you have experience.

## Benefits
* Competitive pay.
* Health insurance.
* Paid time off.
